NYC Protected Streets

What makes a street protected?

I thought it meant less crime. What would you guess?

After resurfacing or reconstruction, a street has a five-year protection period. Openings are restricted, with exceptions for emergencies or work authorized by NYC DOT’s Commissioner.

Protection dates describe pavement maintenance, not neighborhood safety.

About the data

24,435 original rows. One exact duplicate removed, 30 expired records excluded, 24,404 active records mapped. No missing values in the nine original fields.

This is a saved snapshot, not a live inventory. Counts refer to listed street-block records. A missing street does not imply neglect, and a protection end date does not schedule new roadwork. Photos may be older than the protection dates. DOT notes possible errors and omissions.
